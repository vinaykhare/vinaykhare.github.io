Privacy Policy for Workout Minds

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

Vinay Kumar Khare ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Workout Minds mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Workout Minds is built with a Local-First, Privacy-Centric architecture. We do not operate central servers to collect, store, or sell your personal fitness data.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Local Data Storage & Health Metrics

To provide personalized workout plans, the app collects metrics inputted by you. All of the following data is stored locally on your device's internal database:

  • Height, Weight, and calculated BMI.
  • Gender and Primary Fitness Goals.
  • Strength baselines (e.g., max pushups, pull-ups, squats).
  • Detailed workout logs, rest timers, and exercise feedback.

B. Cloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable the Cloud Backup feature, the app will request you to sign in via your Google Account. The app uses the Google Drive API to upload an encrypted backup of your local database to a hidden, app-specific folder (appDataFolder) within your personal Google Drive.

  • We (the developers) do not have the ability to read, view, intercept, or modify the contents of your Google Drive.
  • Your data travels directly from your device to Google's servers.

C. API Keys & AI Generation (BYOK)

The Service utilizes a "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) model for AI generation. If you provide a Google Gemini API Key, it is saved securely in your device's local preferences. It is used exclusively to make direct network requests from your phone to Google's Generative AI servers. We do not transmit or log your API key.

D. Media and Files

If you choose to add custom images to your exercises, the Service may request access to your device's photo gallery. These images are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Workout Minds utilizes specific third-party services that are essential to its core functionality. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): To generate and optimize your custom workout plans, the Service sends your fitness profile (goals, height, weight, strength baseline) and recent workout feedback to Google's Generative AI API using your provided key. Your name and email address are not sent.
  • Google Drive API: Used exclusively for personal cloud backups.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to securely process payments for in-app premium features. We do not collect or store your credit card details.

3. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because Workout Minds is a local-first application, you have absolute control over your data. At any time, you can navigate to Settings -> Danger Zone within the app to:

  1. Delete Cloud Backup: Permanently erase your synced workout database and profile from your personal Google Drive.
  2. Erase All Data: Permanently delete all local SQLite databases, custom routines, and settings from your physical device.
  3. Disconnect Drive: Unlink your Google Account from the app, stopping all future backups.

Uninstalling the application will also delete all locally stored data automatically.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 18 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Disclaimer: Workout Minds is a fitness tracking and planning tool designed for general wellness. It is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program.
